# Region South Visual Validation Checklist

## Critical checks — all must pass

- [ ] The requested display mode is correct.
- [ ] The requested aspect ratio and deliverable type are correct.
- [ ] The subject color family is correct: Math blue, ELA orange, or Science green.
- [ ] Every displayed logo or seal is an authentic supplied asset.
- [ ] No invented logo, seal, badge, crest, or wordmark appears.
- [ ] User-supplied text, numbers, labels, and trends are accurate.
- [ ] Preliminary or embargoed information is labeled exactly as requested.
- [ ] Source and date are included or omitted exactly as requested.
- [ ] Student imagery is grade-appropriate and representative.
- [ ] No required content is missing.

## Design checks

- [ ] The visual has a clear title and information hierarchy.
- [ ] Text is legible, concise, and free of spelling errors.
- [ ] Content blocks are aligned and evenly spaced.
- [ ] Margins and safe areas prevent crowding or clipping.
- [ ] Icons match the subject and share a consistent visual treatment.
- [ ] Decorative patterns are subtle and do not compete with content.
- [ ] The composition resembles the intended approved reference without copying its data.
- [ ] The output feels like a Region South presentation visual rather than a generic school infographic.

## Final decision

- **Pass:** Every critical check passes and no major design problem remains.
- **Revise:** One or more critical checks fail, or the design hierarchy/legibility is materially weak.
- **Reject:** Branding is invented, data is inaccurate, or the output cannot be corrected without rebuilding.
