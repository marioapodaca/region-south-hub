Region South Design System

Create a Region South Graphic with AI

Copy the Region South instructions, open your preferred AI, provide your data, and tell it what you want to create.

Four steps

1

Copy Instructions

Use the master Region South AI instructions below.

2

Open Your AI

Use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, or another capable AI.

3

Provide Your Data

Upload a spreadsheet, PDF, screenshot, image, or paste the values.

4

Describe the Graphic

Tell the AI the subject, audience, data story, and what you want created.

Before you begin

  • □ Data or source file
  • □ Subject: Math, ELA, Science, Attendance, etc.
  • □ Light Mode or Dark Mode
  • □ Source
  • □ Data/download date — or choose to omit it
  • □ Preliminary/embargo status
  • □ Intended audience
  • □ Optional student-image preferences
  • □ Approved logo asset(s), only if branding is required
Not sure which template to use? Ask the AI to recommend RS-01, RS-02, or RS-03 based on the data and purpose.

1. Copy the Region South AI Instructions

The prompt contains the critical rules and points the AI to the public Region South files. If the AI cannot access the linked pages, the embedded rules serve as the fallback.

Open AI Specification

2. Open your preferred AI

ChatGPTPaste the instructions, then upload or paste your data. ClaudePaste the same Region South instructions before describing your graphic. GeminiUse the same instruction block and attach your data if available. Microsoft CopilotPaste the instructions first, then provide the data and request.

3. Tell the AI what you want

Example — Trend graphic
Create a Grade 11 Math DFP trend graphic. Use Dark Mode. Source: CAASPP. Omit the date. The 2026 value is preliminary and embargoed.
Example — Regional comparison
Create a Grade 8 ELA regional comparison using P+A and DFP for East, North, South, and West. Use Light Mode. Source: CAASPP. Omit the date.
Example — Let the AI recommend the layout
I am uploading a spreadsheet with school performance data. Recommend the best Region South template for an executive presentation, explain your recommendation, and wait for my approval before generating the graphic.

How the AI accesses the guide

Most reliable: copy the master instruction block into the AI.

When web access is available: the prompt also directs the AI to the public guide, AI specification, and Quick Start files.

Fallback: if the AI cannot open the URLs, the copied prompt itself contains the critical Region South rules.
