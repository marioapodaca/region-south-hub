Region South Design System

Create a Region South Graphic with AI

Copy the Region South instructions, open your preferred AI, upload your data, and tell it what you want to create.

1. Copy the Region South Instructions

This is the only prompt you need to start. It tells the AI how to follow the Region South visual standards.

View Detailed Style Rules
View full instructions
After you copy:
  1. Open your preferred AI.
  2. Paste the Region South instructions.
  3. Upload your data or file.
  4. Describe the graphic you want.

2. Open Your AI

The same Region South instructions can be used across platforms.

Open ChatGPT Paste the instructions, then upload or paste your data. Open Claude Paste the instructions before describing your graphic. Open Gemini Use the same instructions and attach your data if available. Open Copilot Paste the instructions first, then provide your data and request.

3. Upload Your Data

Give the AI the information it needs to create an accurate graphic.

A

Spreadsheet

Upload Excel, CSV, or another table containing the values you want visualized.

B

PDF or Report

Upload a report when the data or context is already documented in a source file.

C

Screenshot or Image

Use a screenshot when you need the AI to recreate or improve an existing data visual.

D

Paste the Values

For smaller datasets, you can simply paste the numbers into the conversation.

4. Tell the AI What You Want

Your request can be short. The Region South instructions tell the AI what questions to ask if information is missing.

Trend graphic
Create a Grade 11 Math DFP trend graphic. Use Dark Mode. Source: CAASPP. Omit the date. The 2026 value is preliminary and embargoed.
Regional comparison
Create a Grade 8 ELA regional comparison using P+A and DFP for East, North, South, and West. Use Light Mode. Source: CAASPP. Omit the date.
Let the AI recommend the layout
I am uploading a spreadsheet with school performance data. Recommend the best Region South template for an executive presentation, explain your recommendation, and wait for my approval before generating.

Before the Graphic Is Finalized

The AI may ask you to resolve these items before it creates the final visual.

Light or Dark Mode Subject Source Date or omit date Preliminary / Embargo status Audience
You do not need to know every answer before starting. The AI should ask for missing required information instead of inventing it.

Advanced: How the AI Accesses the Guide

Most users do not need this section, but it explains what happens behind the scenes.

Most reliable: the copied Region South instruction block is pasted directly into the AI.

When web access is available: the instructions also point the AI to the public style guide and AI-readable specification.

Fallback: if the AI cannot open the linked files, the copied instructions contain the critical Region South rules.
