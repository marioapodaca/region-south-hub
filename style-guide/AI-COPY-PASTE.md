Before generating a final visual:

1. If I have not specified **Light Mode** or **Dark Mode**, ask me which I want.
   - Light Mode: print, dense charts, detailed comparisons, maximum color contrast.
   - Dark Mode: presentations, executive dashboards, hero graphics, student photography.
   - If I ask for a recommendation, recommend one and wait for my confirmation.

2. If I have not provided a **Source**, ask for it and explicitly offer the option to omit it if it is not pertinent.

3. If I have not provided a **download/data-as-of date**, ask for it and explicitly offer the option to omit it if it is not pertinent.

4. If the data may be preliminary or embargoed and I have not clarified its status, ask before displaying restricted values.

5. Follow these locked subject colors:
   - Math = blue
   - ELA = orange
   - Science / CAST = green

6. When comparing LAUSD regions, use these exact colors and do not deviate:

   Math:
   - South #00237A
   - East #0089FF
   - North #001A5A
   - West #6F8FAF

   ELA:
   - South #FF4D00
   - East #FF8A4C
   - North #B93600
   - West #C97A55

   Science:
   - South #00602D
   - East #5CBF82
   - North #003D1D
   - West #70947F

7. Use **Poppins** as the primary presentation/data typeface.

8. Terminology:
   - Convert PoA to **Proficient + Advanced** or **P+A**.
   - DFP = **Distance from Proficiency**.
   - SP = **Science Points**.
   - If P+A, DFP, or SP appears, define it somewhere in the same visual.

9. Subject icons:
   - Math: calculator, #00237A
   - ELA: open book, LAUSD Orange #FF4D00
   - Science: beaker, flask, or microscope, LAUSD Green #00602D

10. Use authentic logo assets only. Never redraw or approximate the Region South or LAUSD logo. If the logo cannot be inserted reliably, reserve space and add the authentic asset in post-production.

11. Data accuracy takes priority over aesthetics. Verify every numeric label, benchmark, takeaway, and superlative against the supplied data.

12. Do not call ordinary growth "acceleration." Acceleration means the rate of improvement itself is increasing.

13. For preliminary or embargoed values, do not show the value or place a trend endpoint where the value can be inferred. Use a generalized direction-only treatment.

14. Use the most appropriate Region South layout:
   - RS-01 Hero Data: Photo | Main Data | Key Takeaway
   - RS-02 Regional Comparison: P+A | DFP or SP | Region South Story
   - RS-03 Cohort Analysis: Grade Panels + Student Image + Key Takeaway

15. Before finalizing, run a compliance check for:
   - display mode
   - logos
   - subject colors
   - regional colors
   - terminology
   - abbreviation definitions
   - source/date
   - data accuracy
   - accessibility/contrast
   - preliminary/embargo rules

Do not improvise a brand rule when the Region South guide already defines one.
