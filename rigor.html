What This Means

A Problem of Practice names a specific, evidence-based instructional gap that a region or school commits to addressing systematically. Region South's Problem of Practice targets two interconnected issues: the quality and consistency of standards-aligned instruction, and the frequency and structure of student academic discourse — both of which are directly linked to student achievement outcomes.

Theory of Action

Region South's Theory of Action describes the causal logic connecting our regional actions to the outcome we expect for students. It is structured as an "if–then" commitment: when the region takes these five actions, students will receive what they need.

Region South · Theory of Action

When We… Then We Will…

When we,
  1. 1
    Provide teachers with ongoing professional development to deepen their understanding of the standards and alignment of tasks
  2. 2
    Provide opportunities for consistent modeling of structured student discourse strategies
  3. 3
    Support principals in developing systems that provide actionable feedback and reflection that will enhance Tier I instruction
  4. 4
    Support principals to develop systems for frequent progress monitoring
  5. 5
    Build capacity of the instructional leadership team at the school site
↓
Then, we will be able to…

Ensure students receive effective Tier I instruction as well as structured opportunities to engage in academic student discourse — thus reducing the variability and disparity in student achievement across all Region South schools.

Region South · Official Definition

How Region South Defines Rigor

"Rigor is the intentional design of cognitively challenging, inquiry-driven teaching that pushes students into deeper thinking and productive struggle. It involves complex, real-world tasks aligned to grade-level standards that require critical thinking, analysis, and synthesis. Rigor promotes reflection, multiple approaches to problem-solving, and flexible thinking — supporting meaningful learning that transfers beyond the classroom."
For the research foundation behind this definition — Webb's DOK, the Cognitive Rigor Matrix, and Hess (2018) — see Webb's Depth of Knowledge & Cognitive Rigor →
Blueprint for Success — 7 Components
