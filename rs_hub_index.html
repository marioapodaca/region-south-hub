Teaching and Learning Framework (TLF) — 5 Standards

The LAUSD Teaching and Learning Framework describes clear expectations for effective teaching. Based on the work of Charlotte Danielson (2007), revised April 2021. Used as the foundation for teacher evaluation (EDST) and non-classroom teacher evaluation (EDSNCT).

Standard 1
Planning and Preparation
Demonstrating knowledge of content, students, instructional outcomes, and assessment design. The foundation of effective teaching — what teachers know and plan before instruction begins.
1a: Content & Pedagogy 1b: Knowledge of Students 1c: Outcomes 1d: Coherent Instruction 1e: Assessment Design

Standard 2
The Classroom Environment
Creating conditions for learning through respect, rapport, culture for learning, classroom management, and student behavior. The environment within which instruction takes place.

School Leadership Framework (SLF) — Principal Effectiveness
LAUSD School Leadership Framework v14, Revised May 2023. The SLF describes what effective principals do to improve student learning and teacher effectiveness across six standards. Region South uses SLF Standards 1 and 3 most directly in instructional leadership work — Standard 1 for principal professional growth and Standard 3 for instructional leadership, observation practice, and coaching. All six standards are referenced during formal principal evaluation cycles.
SLF Standard 1 · Leadership Focus
Leadership and Professional Growth
Models learning-centered leadership: ethics and judgment, prioritizing high-leverage activities, building observation and coaching skills, reflection, professional development, leading through ambiguity, and cultural proficiency. The standard that governs how principals show up as learners and leaders.
Live View Standard 1
SLF Standard 2
Change Management
Leads and manages change, develops and maintains a shared vision around high expectations for student learning, and creates or maintains a culture of continuous improvement through data-driven goal setting.
Live View Standard 2
SLF Standard 3 · Instructional Leadership · Evaluation Focus
Instruction
Provides support for teachers to improve instruction (3A), promotes quality teaching learning and leadership (3B), and assesses the performance of all staff (3C). Components 3C.1 and 3C.2 — conducting observations and providing actionable feedback — are the unified focus elements for principal evaluation and the core of instructional supervision work.
Live View Standard 3
SLF Standard 4
Culture of Learning and Positive Behavior
Creates or maintains cultures conducive to educator growth and student growth, including positive school climate, equitable practices, CLRP, student motivation, courageous conversations about bias, and social-emotional learning.
Live View Standard 4
SLF Standard 5
Family and Community Engagement
Engages families and community members as partners in student success, provides opportunities for family and community education, establishes community partnerships, and creates or maintains school-home-community communication structures.
Live View Standard 5
SLF Standard 6
Systems and Operations
Manages people, time and resources; creates or maintains a productive school environment; and demonstrates legal and policy compliance to promote student learning in a safe and productive environment.
